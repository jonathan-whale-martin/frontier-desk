How we use AI

{{ site.name }} uses AI to help us cover AI news quickly. Here is exactly how.

Our system checks official company blogs and trusted news outlets several times a day. When it finds a new AI story, an AI model writes a short summary using only those sources. A second AI check compares every claim in the draft against the sources and flags anything it cannot find there.

A human editor then reads every draft before it is published. Drafts with problems are fixed or dropped. Nothing is published automatically.

Every article lists and links to its sources, so you can read the original reporting. We write summaries in our own words and do not copy other outlets' articles.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we correct it and note the change at the bottom of the article. Email {{ site.contact_email }} to report a mistake.

Advertising and affiliate links

We show ads and some articles contain affiliate links, which earn us a commission if you buy. Sponsored content is always labelled. Advertisers never see articles before they are published and do not influence our coverage.
