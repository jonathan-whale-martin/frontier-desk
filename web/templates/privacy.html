What we collect and why

Newsletter. If you sign up, we collect your email address so we can send you the newsletter. We rely on your consent, and you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email. Our newsletter is sent by Buttondown, which stores the list for us.

Website visits. Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records technical information such as your IP address when you visit, to keep the service running and secure. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.

Article counter. To count your free articles each month, the site stores a short list of the articles you have read in your own browser (local storage). It stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can clear it by clearing your browser data.

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Other information is kept only as long as it is needed for the purposes above.
