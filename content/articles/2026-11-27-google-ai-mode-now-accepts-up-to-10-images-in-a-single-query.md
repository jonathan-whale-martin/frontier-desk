Google AI Mode now lets users take several photos or upload several images for a single query, according to Search Engine Roundtable.

Users can attach up to 10 images to one AI Mode query, and Google responds taking all of them into account, the site reported.

The feature was first spotted by Android Authority. It reportedly replaces the camera's shutter button with a plus icon, so users can capture multiple photos in one go. Android Authority said the change is rolling out with the latest version of the Google app.

Search Engine Roundtable said it tried the feature on an iPhone using the Google app and that it appeared to work. The site also shared a screenshot of an AI Mode response. The report follows an earlier image collections feature in AI Mode.

Search Engine Roundtable described the update as part of Google's broader push into multimodal and AI search. Neither source quoted an official Google statement on the rollout, and the details come from third-party reporting and testing.
