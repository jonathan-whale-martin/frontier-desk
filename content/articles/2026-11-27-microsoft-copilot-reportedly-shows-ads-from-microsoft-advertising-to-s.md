Search Engine Roundtable reports that Microsoft is showing ads in Copilot responses to some users, powered by Microsoft Advertising.

The site's author said the practice is not new and that they had seen examples earlier this year, but this is the first time they have covered it.

The report is based on a screenshot of a Copilot response containing ads, which a user shared on X. The author said they could not reproduce the result after trying a dozen or so queries.

The author also noted that Microsoft already shows ads in Bing AI Overviews and Copilot Search, so ads in AI responses are not new for the company.

The article also compares the situation with Google. According to Search Engine Roundtable, Google has been showing more ads in AI Mode, including new multiturn ads, and has expanded them to more regions. Google has said twice that it has no plans for ads in Gemini.

Microsoft has not commented in the source material, and the article does not say how widely the ads are shown.
