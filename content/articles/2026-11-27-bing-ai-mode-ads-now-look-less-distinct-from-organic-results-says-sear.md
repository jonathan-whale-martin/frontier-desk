Search Engine Roundtable reports that ads in Bing AI Mode results are harder to tell apart from organic results. According to the site, the ads are no longer outlined and look more blended into the organic response.

The report cites examples posted by Khushal Bherwani on SEW. Search Engine Roundtable said that previously the ads looked similar to an example it had published earlier.

The site noted that earlier this year, Bing used almost transparent sponsored labels. It said Microsoft had responded to similar complaints about hard-to-see ad labels in the past. Search Engine Roundtable also said it expects this behaviour to continue, including within AI Mode and Copilot Search.

The source is a single trade-news report based on a forum discussion and user-posted examples. Microsoft has not commented in the source material, and the report does not describe an official change announced by Microsoft Advertising.
