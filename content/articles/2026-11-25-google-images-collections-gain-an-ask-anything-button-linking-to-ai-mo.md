Google has added an "Ask anything" button to the collections and saved items section of Google Images, according to Search Engine Roundtable. The button takes all the images in a collection and lets users ask Google AI Mode about them in one go.

Search Engine Roundtable reported that users type a question, which is grouped with the collection's images before they are taken into AI Mode. The site also said the feature suggests questions to ask.

The change was first spotted by Damien Andell, who posted a video about it on X on 22 November 2026. In his post, he noted that AI Mode's source analysis shows a "From your saved items" label marked "Preferred".

The sources reviewed do not include an official statement from Google about the feature, so details such as rollout scope and availability are not confirmed. Users can check for the button in their own Google Image collections and saved items section.
