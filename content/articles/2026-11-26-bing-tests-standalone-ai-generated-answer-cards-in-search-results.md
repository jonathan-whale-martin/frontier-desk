Microsoft Bing is testing a new style of AI-generated answer, according to Search Engine Roundtable. The answers carry an "AI-Generated answer" label and appear to be distinct from the AI Overviews and Copilot Search boxes already in Bing.

The card was first spotted by a user named Venkat, who posted about it on SEW. He described it as a standalone card in mobile results, sitting apart from the regular search listings.

Search Engine Roundtable said it was able to reproduce the card on both mobile and desktop. The label text says the summary was generated using AI from online sources, and that users can view the original source information through "Learn more" links.

Search Engine Roundtable said it did not know why Bing was presenting these answers differently from its existing AI features. The source does not say how widely the test is running, and Microsoft has not commented in the article.

The report is based on observations of the feature rather than an official announcement.
