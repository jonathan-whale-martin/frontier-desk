OpenAI has sent out a product update on ChatGPT Ads, according to Search Engine Roundtable, which reported the changes on 25 November 2026.

The main changes are:

Business Agents outside the US: Eligible ecommerce and online service businesses in supported markets outside the US can now create and publish a Business Agent in the ChatGPT Business Hub. Advertisers who meet ad policy requirements can also add their agent to a campaign in Ads Manager as a Sponsored Business Agent.

New audience metrics: Advertisers can now measure Reach, meaning how many unique people saw an ad at least once, and Frequency, the average number of times those people saw it. Further metrics include Click Reach, Total Reach, Unique CTR, Unique CPC and Cost per 1,000 Reached. These are available in Ads Manager and the Ads API.

"Learn more" link: Single image ads now show a "Learn more" link below the description by default. It points to the landing page already set in Ads Manager, so no extra setup is needed.

Other changes: Advertisers can hide sale prices in a product-feed ad group so the regular price shows again when a promotion ends. Ads Manager also now shows more context on which policy applies when an ad is not approved. OpenAI said the guidelines for approving ads have not changed.
