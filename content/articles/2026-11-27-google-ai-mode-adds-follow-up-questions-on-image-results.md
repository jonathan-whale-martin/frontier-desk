Google's AI Mode now allows users to ask a follow-up question about image results, according to Search Engine Roundtable.

The feature appears after a user clicks an image within AI Mode. This opens a panel on the right side showing the image, with a follow-up option at the bottom.

Barry Schwartz, founder of Search Engine Roundtable, said he has seen two versions of the button. One reads "Ask a follow-up" and has an animated Google ring around it. The other reads "Ask about this image."

Khushal Bherwani first spotted the "Ask about this image" button and shared a GIF of it on Search Engine Roundtable, Schwartz wrote.

The source does not say whether Google has announced the feature, when it will roll out widely, or how the follow-up answers are generated. Only the two button variants and the panel placement are described.
