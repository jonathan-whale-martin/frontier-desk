Google announced Create My Widget at its I/O event in the spring, according to The Verge. The feature is part of Android's "Gemini Intelligence" suite, is in beta, and has only just started rolling out to Pixel phones.

The Verge's reviewer tried it on a Pixel 11 Pro. Creating a simple widget took around 20 seconds, and the reviewer made widgets that show rotating film quotes and display a message when it is raining.

According to The Verge, many widgets work as shortcuts that open the Gemini interface on the home screen, which then generates a response. Templates cover things like holiday countdowns and sports team scores. Open-ended requests draw on Google search results or prompt Gemini directly.

The reviewer's attempt to build a widget showing real-time bus arrivals did not work. The widget produced a tile that used Gemini to search for arrival times, and Gemini returned a general answer about how often the bus runs based on the schedule rather than live data.

The Verge described the feature as more of a one-size-fits-all tool than the customisable mini apps the reviewer had hoped for. The article does not say how widely the feature is available beyond Pixel phones.
