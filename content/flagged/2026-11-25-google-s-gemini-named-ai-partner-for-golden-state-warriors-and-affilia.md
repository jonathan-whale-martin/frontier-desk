Google has widened its tie-up with Golden State, naming Gemini the Official AI Assistant Partner and Official Generative AI and Enterprise LLM Partner of the Warriors, the 2026 WNBA champion Valkyries, and Chase Center.

According to Google, the partnership reaches across the entire franchise. Fans attending games will see more interactive experiences, including features on the interactive videoboards inside Chase Center, while supporters watching from home will get the same boost through digital channels.

Internally, Golden State is using Gemini for business to create bespoke AI agents intended to make staff more productive in both commercial and basketball operations.

Google also points out that Golden State has been a Founding Partner of Chase Center since 2019 and has previously relied on the company's generative AI tools to keep its operations running smoothly.

The information comes from a Google blog post, so it reflects Google's own description of the deal. The post gives no financial terms and no measured outcomes from the custom agents.
